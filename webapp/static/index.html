What does this system do?

Instead of summarizing papers independently, this system retrieves a set of relevant papers, extracts their structured contributions, and compares them — surfacing overlaps, unique contributions, and methodological gaps that existing tools miss.

Explore the structured results

Results include: per-paper contribution summaries, explicit paper-level overlaps, differentiating aspects per paper, a problem × method gap matrix showing absent combinations, and a synthesis paragraph with citations.

⚠️ Corpus note: The current corpus contains 100 papers in the agentic AI domain. Results are corpus-level observations only — gap findings reflect what is absent from this retrieved set, not the broader literature.
